<h3 class="small-padded-bottom  center-text">Below are the questions we have been asked about our dances</h3>
<h5><strong>Do I need a partner?</strong></h5>
<h6>Although it is open to anybody, in practice, only those with partners or a group attended so if you come on your own, you won't get a dance.  </h6>
<h5><strong>Will there be Bus Stop/Taxi/Queue Dances/Snowballs?</strong></h5>
<h6>No</h6>
<h5><strong>Will there be Sequence Dancing?</strong></h5>
<h6>Usually there are 40 Ballroom and Latin with 10 Sequence dances spread throughtout the evening. </h6>
<h5><strong>What level of dancers are you expecting or targeting?</strong></h5>
<h6>Social Dancers of any standard are welcome, as long as you like Ballroom and Latin dancing it does not matter if you are complete beginners or at Gold or beyond. However, it is a social dance and not for those that want to practice for competitions </h6>
<h5><strong>Where is the hall?</strong></h5>
<h6> Woodmansterne Village Hall, Carshalton Road (opposite 34), Woodmansterne, SM7 3HR <a href="http://streetmap.co.uk/grid/527573_160104/" target="_blank" >Show Map</a></h6>
  <h6>It is 1 mile from Banstead, 2 miles from Coulsdon, 3 from Purley, 4 from Sutton, 5 from Epsom and 6 from Croydon</h6>
<h5><strong>Is there parking?</strong></h5>
<h6>There is large car park the entrance is opposite number 34.</h6>
